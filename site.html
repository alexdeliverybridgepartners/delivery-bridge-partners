Plan a PMO setup

PMO Improvement Sprint

A focused four-to-six-week sprint, usually about a month. We measure how stakeholders rate your completed projects using PMI’s Net Project Success Score, compare estimated against actual schedule and budget, review open change orders, and assess whether your team has the capacity, roles and skills to deliver the portfolio you’ve approved.

PMI’s research found that projects with a measurement system in place averaged a Net Project Success Score of 45, against 6 for projects without one.

You get an NPSS Baseline, a PMO Intelligence Dashboard and a Delivery Improvement Plan, so leadership can make intake, prioritization and resourcing decisions with confidence.

Best for

  • Projects that finish on paper but disappoint stakeholders
  • Estimates that keep drifting from approved schedules and budgets
  • Project managers stretched across too many projects or roles
